The game

Run, grab the cheese, get out. Then do it faster.

You're a hamster in a maze that was built to stop you. Grab the cheese, find the gate, and keep moving — something is always closing in behind you.

Death isn't a punishment here, it's rhythm. You're back in half a second, no menus, no waiting, straight into the next run.

Studio

A small studio, building for the phone in your hand.

İdiqut Games makes games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. No clutter, no noise — a clear idea, tuned until it feels right, and nothing else in the way.

The name comes from İdiqut, the title carried by the rulers of the Uyghur Kingdom of Qocho — roughly sacred fortune. The mark keeps that origin, and puts it in front of a screen.

Small by design

We ship focused games, not feature lists. One idea, done properly, beats ten half-finished ones.

Feel comes first

Controls, timing and feedback get tuned before anything else. If it doesn't feel good in the hand, it isn't finished.

Runs anywhere

Built to be light and fast on the phone you already own — not just on this year's flagship.
