01 — Studio

An independent studio, building for the phone in your hand.

İdiqut Games makes games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. No clutter, no noise — a clear idea, tuned until it feels right, and nothing else in the way.

The name comes from İdiqut, the title carried by the rulers of the Uyghur Kingdom of Qocho — roughly sacred fortune. The mark keeps that origin: ink on paper, a lone standard-bearer, and a banner still flying.
